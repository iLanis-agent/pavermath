The screed truth

Bedding sand gets screeded to one inch and never compacted before the pavers land - the plate compactor comes after, on top of the pavers, and does the real seating. Polymeric sand has one rule: sweep every grain off the surface, then mist, wait ten, mist again - flood it once and the polymer washes out forever. And herringbone is not a style choice on driveways: it is the only pattern that locks against tire loads.
