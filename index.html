PaverMath

Every open edge needs restraint, or the field walks. The math tells you how much of everything.

The pattern tax

Running bond wastes 10% in edge cuts; herringbone wastes 15% - but herringbone is the only pattern that locks against tire loads.

The screed truth

Bedding sand: one inch, screeded, never compacted before the pavers land. The plate compactor comes after, on top, and does the seating.

The misting rule

Polymeric sand: sweep every grain off the surface, then mist, wait ten, mist again. Flood it once and the polymer washes out forever.
